Privacy Policy for One dB

Effective date: September 13, 2026

One dB is a sound-level estimation tool for Android. This policy explains how the app uses microphone access, stores measurement history, and works with Google advertising, consent, and billing services.

Microphone access

One dB requests RECORD_AUDIO solely to calculate an approximate sound level in real time. Microphone samples are processed in memory and discarded after each calculation. The app does not create or save audio recordings, upload microphone audio, send it to advertising providers, or use it for speech recognition or voice identification. The meter cannot operate without permission, which can be revoked in Android settings.

Measurement history and exports

One dB Pro can save a user-entered title and note, date and time, duration, minimum, average and maximum estimated dB values, threshold-exceedance summaries, and sampled calculated dB values. These calculated numbers—not audio—are stored in the app's private local database. There is no One dB account, cloud sync, or developer-operated server.

CSV files and measurement image cards are generated locally only when the user chooses Share or Export. Android's system share sheet lets the user choose a destination. Any copy sent there is handled under that destination's privacy practices. One dB does not upload exports automatically.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for a banner; Pro removes it. Google Mobile Ads may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent signals depending on region, settings, privacy choices, and production configuration. This data is not microphone audio.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) obtains consent information and displays privacy choices when required. It may process consent status and related device or request information. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google partner-services explanation.

Google Play Billing

Google Play Billing processes the optional onedb_pro_lifetime purchase. The app receives product information, localized price, purchase status, and a purchase token to provide and restore Pro. One dB does not receive full payment-card details or send purchase data to its own server.

Google Play In-App Review

One dB may ask Google Play to present its standard in-app review flow after locally stored eligibility conditions based on install age, app launches, completed measurements, recent failures, and the last request time are met. Ratings and review text are entered into and processed by Google Play. One dB does not receive a signal indicating whether the review card appeared or whether a rating or review was submitted.

User choices, retention, and security

Users can deny or revoke microphone access, use UMP privacy options when available, manage Google advertising settings, buy Pro to remove the banner, delete saved measurements, or uninstall the app. Local history remains until deletion, app-data clearing, or uninstall, subject to Android backup and restore. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children

One dB is a general utility and is not specifically directed to children. The Play Console target audience and AdMob settings must be configured consistently before publication.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be revised for material changes.
